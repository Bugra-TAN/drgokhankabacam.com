About

Prof. Dr. Gökhan Kabaçam

Gastroenterology and Hepatology Specialist

Prof. Dr. Gökhan Kabaçam graduated from the English-Taught Medical Program at Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine in 2001. He completed his residency in Internal Medicine and fellowship training in Gastroenterology at Ankara University Faculty of Medicine.

Throughout his academic and clinical career, he has focused particularly on hepatology, liver transplantation, cirrhosis, advanced liver disease, liver cancer, and advanced diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy.

Between 2015 and 2026, Prof. Dr. Kabaçam worked at Ankara Güven Hospital, serving as Head of the Department of Gastroenterology during the final three years of this period. He also served as a transplant hepatologist in the Liver Transplant Program, taking an active role in pre-transplant evaluation, the transplant process, and post-transplant follow-up.

In 2021, he was appointed Professor at Yüksek İhtisas University Faculty of Medicine. He has held active roles in national and international professional organizations; He has carried out numerous activities representing Türkiye within the International Liver Transplantation Society (ILTS) and has organized various meetings and courses. He is the former Chair of the ILTS Membership and Communications Committee.

His scientific work has received international recognition. Prof. Dr. Kabaçam has received the Young Investigator Award four times from the European Association for the Study of the Liver (EASL). He has 64 scientific articles published in national and international journals and an H-index of 19.
